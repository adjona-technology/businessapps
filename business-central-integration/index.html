Business Central integration

Connect sales and finance around the information they need to share

Dynamics 365 Sales and Business Central can exchange agreed information through Dataverse. We help define and configure that connection around your sales-to-finance handover, with clear record ownership and practical tests.

The aim is to reduce repeated administration and make the next step easier to manage.

Discuss your sales-to-finance handover

Start with the point where responsibility changes

When is an opportunity ready for finance? Who confirms the customer details? Which products or services are involved? Who resolves a missing or mismatched record?

These decisions shape the integration. We work through them with the people responsible for the process, then translate the agreed requirements into configuration and tests.

A focused integration engagement

The work can include:

  • Mapping the intended sales-to-finance handover.
  • Reviewing the relevant environments, access and licensing dependencies.
  • Agreeing the records, fields and synchronisation directions.
  • Defining how records are matched and coupled.
  • Configuring the agreed integration.
  • Testing representative changes and exceptions.
  • Documenting routine checks and responsibility for resolving issues.

What you receive

The agreed integration configuration, a record of the field mappings, synchronisation directions and matching rules, and the results of testing against the agreed examples. We walk through the process with the people responsible and document the routine checks and who resolves issues.

Questions

Common questions

What will change for your team?

That depends on the handover we agree to improve. It might mean selected customer information reaching the next system, a clearer point for finance to take over, or a documented way to identify and resolve synchronisation issues.

The proposal states the expected behaviour and how we will verify it, giving everyone the same definition of completion.

Does this include a complete Business Central rollout?

This service focuses on the CRM connection and the agreed operational handover. A wider finance implementation, accounting design or migration is scoped separately with the appropriate finance expertise.

Will the integration remove duplicate entry?

It can reduce repeated entry for the information included in the integration. We identify the relevant records and fields, then test that the intended information reaches the right place and behaves correctly when updated.
