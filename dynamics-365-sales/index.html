Dynamics 365 Sales

Give every enquiry an owner and every opportunity a next step

Dynamics 365 Sales helps teams manage leads, opportunities and customer relationships. We configure it around the way your organisation handles new enquiries and develops work, so people know what needs attention and who is responsible.

Discuss your sales process

Make follow-up easier to manage

A CRM becomes useful when the team can answer a few straightforward questions: who owns this enquiry, what has happened, what is the next action and when is it due?

We help define those working rules and translate them into the relevant records, stages, forms and views in Dynamics 365 Sales.

What the work covers

  • Enquiry capture and lead assignment.
  • Account, contact and opportunity records.
  • Clear stages and the information needed at each handover.
  • Views that help people find their next action.
  • Follow-up activities and selected notifications.
  • Data checks and reporting requirements.
  • An agreed handover towards Business Central where required.

Relevant to charities as well as businesses

A charity may need to manage paid training enquiries, service contracts or partnership opportunities. A smaller business may need a consistent way to handle prospective customers and quotations.

For a care provider, this might mean managing business enquiries and the administrative handover to accounts.

The terminology and stages should reflect the work your team actually does. We agree those requirements before configuration.

What you receive

The agreed configuration, a walkthrough of the process and documentation of the important decisions and checks. We define how completion will be assessed before implementation begins.

Questions

Common questions

Can you improve an existing setup?

Yes. We can review an existing Dynamics 365 Sales process to identify where configuration, record quality or unclear responsibilities are getting in the way, then propose a focused improvement.

Is Dynamics 365 Sales affordable for a small organisation?

It can be, but the right choice depends on the users, features and integrations you need. Compare the edition and total cost, including configuration, support and any Business Central or Power Platform licences required.

Compare Microsoft's current UK pricing for Sales Professional and Sales Enterprise, including the payment commitment and VAT treatment. Edition capabilities differ, so the lowest-priced edition is not automatically suitable for the proposed integration.

Eligible nonprofits may qualify for discounted Sales Enterprise licensing. Microsoft validates eligibility, and the available UK offer must be confirmed for your organisation. Being a charity or care provider does not automatically establish eligibility. See Microsoft's nonprofit offers.

If you need a simpler record-and-follow-up process, a tailored Power Apps approach may be a better fit. We compare the requirements and full cost before recommending a route; Power Apps is not automatically cheaper.

Read: Power Apps or Dynamics 365 Sales?

Can the information connect to finance?

Dynamics 365 Sales can connect to Business Central through Dataverse. We define which records and fields should move, how they match and what needs testing.
