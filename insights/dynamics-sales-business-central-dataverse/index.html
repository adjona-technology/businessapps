How Dynamics 365 Sales connects to Business Central through Dataverse

By Adjona Business Applications · Published 2 November 2026

Dynamics 365 Sales and Business Central can exchange agreed information through Dataverse. The integration connects customer-facing work with finance, while record mappings and synchronisation settings determine what information moves and how it behaves.

The starting point is the handover your organisation needs.

What does each application do?

Dynamics 365 Sales supports leads, opportunities, customer relationships and sales activity. Business Central supports business management, including finance. Dataverse is the data platform involved in connecting the applications.

For a project, identify the specific records and fields the next team needs. This turns a broad request to "connect the systems" into something that can be configured and checked.

Which system owns the information?

Customer details may be used by both sales and finance. Agree where important information is maintained, which changes should synchronise and who resolves conflicting values.

For example, an address change should have a defined route through the process. The people responsible need to know where to make the change and how to confirm it has reached the other application.

How do the records match?

The intended records need to be associated across the systems. Existing entries, duplicate records and inconsistent identifiers can make that work harder.

Use representative examples to verify matching and coupling. Check both a new record and a change to an existing one, with the expected result recorded before testing.

What should an integration test cover?

Test the handover people will rely on. Does the intended record appear? Are its values correct? What happens after an update? Can someone recognise and resolve a failure?

Where invoicing is part of the requirement, include the relevant finance steps and confirmations in the test plan. This gives the sales and finance teams a shared view of completion.

What should you prepare for a review?

Bring an example of the handover, the information collected by sales, the information finance needs and the people responsible for decisions. That is enough to begin discussing the requirement.
