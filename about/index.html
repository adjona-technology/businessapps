About Adjona Business Applications

Adjona Business Applications helps charities, care providers and smaller businesses improve how enquiries are managed and how customer information passes from sales to finance.

We review the process, configure the agreed solution and explain the result, with responsibilities agreed at each stage.

Practical Microsoft technology with a governance perspective

Our work combines Dynamics 365 Sales, Business Central integration and Power Platform with attention to how organisations operate.

Our approach draws on practical charity governance and data protection experience, connecting operational requirements with information ownership, access and accountability.

Part of Adjona Technology Limited

Adjona Business Applications is a trading name of Adjona Technology Limited, incorporated on 6 January 2004. Adjona Technology is a Microsoft AI Cloud Partner, with wider consultancy work in Microsoft 365 security, governance and automation.

That gives this service a particular focus: make the handover work, with appropriate attention to access, accountability and ongoing administration.
