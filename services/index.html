Services

A clearer route from the first enquiry to the finance handover

We help charities and smaller businesses improve enquiry ownership, sales follow-up and the exchange of information between CRM and finance.

The core services are Dynamics 365 Sales configuration and Business Central integration. We use Power Platform and reporting tools where they support the agreed process.

Start with a fixed-fee process review

A review gives you a practical starting point when the problem is clear but the solution is not.

We select one process, such as enquiry-to-opportunity or sales-to-finance, and agree the review scope and fee before work begins. We examine the current steps, systems, records and responsibilities, then set out the options and recommended next action.

You receive:

  • A summary of the current process and the points causing difficulty.
  • Recommended improvements and the reasons behind them.
  • Relevant data, access, licensing and integration dependencies.
  • A proposed first stage, with implementation quoted separately.

Dynamics 365 Sales configuration

Give your enquiries, customer records and opportunities a consistent structure. We help define ownership, stages, working views and follow-up so the system supports the way you manage relationships and new work.

Explore Dynamics 365 Sales consultancy

Dynamics 365 Sales and Business Central integration

Define the information that needs to pass between sales and finance, configure the agreed connection through Dataverse and test the relevant records and handovers.

The work centres on practical questions: which system maintains customer details, how records match and who responds when something needs attention.

Explore Business Central integration

Supporting the process with Power Platform

Some steps need a tailored form, structured operational record or automated notification. We use Dataverse, Power Apps and Power Automate where they help complete the agreed workflow.

That might mean capturing an enquiry in a consistent format, assigning a record or notifying someone that an action is due. The design includes ownership and a way to recognise failures.

Reporting that supports follow-up

A useful report helps someone decide what to do next. Power BI can bring agreed CRM and operational measures into a clearer view of activity, ownership and exceptions.

Reporting is scoped around the questions your team needs to answer and the quality of the available data.

How pricing works

The initial conversation is free. Process reviews have an agreed fixed fee. Configuration and integration work are quoted against a defined scope, with software licences and other third-party costs identified separately where applicable.

You can commission a useful first stage before deciding whether to extend the work.
