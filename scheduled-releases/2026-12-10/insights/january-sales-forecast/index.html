What earns an opportunity a place in your January forecast?

By Adjona Business Applications · Published 10 December 2026

A December pipeline review is more useful when every expected sale has a next step the buyer has actually agreed.

Two proposals went out on the same day for the same amount. One buyer has arranged a meeting with their finance lead on 7 January. The other acknowledged receipt six weeks ago and has given no decision date. A pipeline sorted by proposal value makes them look alike. A January staffing decision should treat them differently.

Read the last exchange before moving the date

For each proposal, ask what the buyer has committed to do, who is involved and when the next conversation will happen. Separate the buyer's commitment from your own reminder. "Call on 7 January" is a task for the salesperson. "Buyer has invited finance to a decision meeting on 7 January" is evidence about the buying process. Even that meeting may end without a sale.

Agree what qualifies a deal for the forecast. A small team might include an opportunity when there is an identified need, a plausible decision window and a dated next step agreed with the buyer. It can still retain a genuinely open conversation outside that forecast. Set a review date and record what would bring it back in.

Use a pipeline meeting to make one decision per record

Ask whether the team should prepare for a decision, make a specific follow-up or stop allocating near-term capacity. Record the reason in a sentence another colleague could understand. Replace an estimated close date only when there is a reason for the new date. Rolling every December date into January creates activity for the administrator and little information for the business.

Apply the same discipline at the start of the pipeline, and first make sure every enquiry arrived (see Did every website enquiry reach Dynamics 365?). An enquiry asking for information is evidence of interest. Qualification is where the team decides whether it has a genuine sales opportunity. Dynamics 365 Sales supports that step, with record creation depending on the organisation's configuration. Microsoft Learn: Qualify and convert a lead to opportunity

At the end of the review, ask which January commitments you would be prepared to reserve delivery time for. The answer is a more useful forecast than the total value of every proposal still marked open.
