Connect enquiries, sales and finance, with clearer ownership at every step

Adjona Business Applications helps charities, care providers and smaller businesses organise their sales process and improve the handover to finance using Dynamics 365 Sales and Business Central.

Where does the handover break down?

An enquiry arrives, but nobody owns the follow-up. A promising opportunity stays in someone's inbox. Finance asks for customer details that have already been collected. Preparing a progress report means checking several spreadsheets.

We help you define a clearer route through that work: what is recorded, who takes the next action and which information needs to reach the next person or system.

Two connected areas of work

01

Give enquiries and opportunities a clear next step

Configure Dynamics 365 Sales around how your organisation handles enquiries, relationships and opportunities. Make ownership visible, capture useful information and give people a practical way to manage follow-up.

Explore Dynamics 365 Sales consultancy
02

Improve the handover from sales to finance

Connect agreed information between Dynamics 365 Sales and Business Central through Dataverse. Define which system maintains each record, test how changes move and make the handover easier to manage.

Explore Business Central integration

Practical delivery with clear accountability

Each project has an agreed scope, clear responsibilities on both sides and defined checks for completion. You know who is accountable for each part of the work and how the result will be handed over. Where the requirement is not yet clear, a fixed-fee process review defines it before any configuration begins.

Our approach combines practical application configuration with charity governance and data protection experience. Alongside making the process work, we consider who can access the information, who maintains it and how your organisation will manage it after handover.

A process that fits your organisation

For a charity, the starting point might be a training enquiry, a partnership opportunity or a paid-service booking that needs an owner and a clear route to finance.

For a care provider or smaller business, it might be a business enquiry that moves between administration, management and accounts, with the same details requested at each stage.

The first step is to understand that handover. Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI can support the process where a tailored screen, notification or report is useful.
