How projects work

From agreed scope to documented handover

We keep projects focused on an operational outcome: clearer ownership, a better-defined handover or information reaching the people who need it.

Here is how we move from the problem to an agreed piece of work.

The process

Five stages

  1. 1

    Step 1: Talk through the requirement

    The introductory conversation is free and lasts 30 minutes. We discuss your current process, where the difficulty occurs and what you want to improve.

    You leave knowing whether we can help and what the next step would involve. If the requirement needs further investigation, we explain what a focused review would cover.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Agree a review or implementation scope

    A process review has a fixed fee agreed before work starts. It gives you written findings, options and recommended next steps.

    Where the requirement is sufficiently defined, we can prepare a proposal for implementation. The proposal identifies deliverables, responsibilities, dependencies, fees and the checks used to assess completion.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Configure and connect

    We carry out the agreed application, workflow or integration work. Access arrangements and the development or testing environment are agreed before changes begin.

    You provide a process owner and the people needed to confirm operational or finance requirements.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Test the real handover

    We use agreed examples to check that information reaches the intended place, values are correct and the next person can take the required action.

    The checks also cover relevant exceptions, so the team understands what happens when a record or workflow needs attention.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Walk through and hand over

    We explain the resulting process and provide the documentation agreed in the proposal. This includes the important configuration decisions, dependencies and routine checks.

    Further changes or support can then be agreed as a separate piece of work.

Free introductory conversation

Start with a manageable first stage

A first stage could focus on one enquiry route, one CRM-to-finance handover or one operational report. You can assess the result before extending the scope.
